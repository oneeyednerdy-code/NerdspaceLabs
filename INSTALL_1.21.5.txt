IMPORTANT TEST
1. Use a private browser window.
2. Open Nerdspace.
3. Click Log in with Twitch.
4. You should be redirected into Twitch OAuth instead of remaining indefinitely on Building your creator workspace.
5. Complete Twitch authorization.
6. Confirm Nerdspace returns and validates the token.
7. Confirm the populated workspace opens.
8. Download a diagnostic log if it does not.

A healthy diagnostic sequence should now include auth events such as:
- login button clicked
- beginLogin invoked
- oauth callback checked
- validating token
- token valid
