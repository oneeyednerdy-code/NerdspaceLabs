TWITCH VISUAL DATA
- Profile requests now cover the union of followed and currently-live creator IDs.
- Live creator records carry Twitch profile image, offline image, stream thumbnail and game box art data.
- Stream thumbnail placeholders are converted to 640x360 URLs.
- Game box-art placeholders are converted to 285x380 URLs.
- Creator cards use profile image first and game art as fallback.
- Live stream preview images are displayed on creator cards.
- Data Status reports profile image, stream image and game art counts.
- CSP now permits Twitch/JTVNW CDN subdomains.

SCHEDULE OVERLAP
- Rebuilt overlap around weekly weekday/time windows instead of exact calendar dates.
- Published Twitch schedules are preferred.
- Observed VOD timing is fallback evidence.
- Matching measures direct weekly overlap, shared weekdays, start-time proximity and evidence confidence.
- Creator Match displays readable overlap such as: 2h 30m overlap • Wed, Fri.
- A recurring Wednesday schedule can match another Wednesday schedule even when Twitch returned occurrences from different weeks.
