NERDSPACE LABS 1.21.1 — COMPACT DISCOVERY

COMPACT UI
- Reduced vertical padding and card density across major workspace surfaces.
- Long creator/result collections use bounded desktop result regions instead of forcing the entire page to grow.
- Detailed Schedule Lab controls move into a collapsible advanced section.

SCHEDULE MATCH / SOLSTICE
- Schedule Lab is now follower-first.
- Search followed creators and filter by minimum weekly overlap, day, genre and live state.
- Sort by best overlap, creator name or current viewers.
- Existing detailed weekly matrix and Find My Windows remain available under an expandable section.

NETWORK EXPLORER
- Added creator/game/tag search.
- Added relationship filters: all, following, saved, potential collabs, raid later.
- Added genre and live/offline filters.
- Compact responsive result grid replaces an unnecessarily long scrolling list.

GENRES
- Expanded creator genre taxonomy.
- MMO is now a first-class genre.
- Added RPG, Action, Adventure, Shooter, FPS, Battle Royale, MOBA, Strategy, Simulation, Survival, Horror, Sports, Racing, Fighting, Platformer, Puzzle, Rhythm, Card & Board, Sandbox, Crafting, Co-op, Party, Retro, Indie, Creative, Music, Art, Just Chatting and IRL.
- Added IGDB aliases including Massively Multiplayer/MMORPG -> MMO.

NERDSYNC DISCOVERY
- Added discovery-source controls: Discovery Feed, Following, Outside My Network.
- Discovery pool can merge followed live creators with existing NerdSync/category/search discovery results when available.
- This removes the follower-only assumption from discovery-oriented surfaces while keeping Following as an explicit filter.

PRE-D1
- No D1 storage.
- Existing local preferences/presets retained.
