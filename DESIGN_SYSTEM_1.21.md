# Nerdspace Labs 1.21
Nerdspace is a unified creator workspace, not a dashboard of mini-apps.

## Structure
Discover → Plan → Network → Analyze → System.

## Principles
- Decisions before metrics.
- Creator-controlled filters and weights.
- Explain evidence rather than promise a magic algorithm.
- Twitch is core; TwitchTracker/IGDB are supplemental and failures remain isolated.
- Near-black surfaces, Twitch-adjacent violet, compact typography, restrained space styling.
- Presets/preferences are browser-local until D1.
